## 👋 About Me

I'm **Phillip Green**, a full-stack developer, Quality Engineer, and founder of **[Green Digital Solutions](https://www.greendigitalsolutions.dev/)** in Kentucky.

My background sits at the intersection of **software development, engineering, and continuous improvement**.

Before I was building applications and websites, I was solving problems in manufacturing — analyzing processes, identifying failure points, improving systems, and finding practical ways to make things work better.

That mindset never really changed.

Now I apply it to software.

I care about clean interfaces, sensible architecture, maintainable code, and building things that solve an actual problem instead of adding complexity for the sake of it.

**Developer** → Next.js, React, TypeScript, APIs, databases  
**Engineer** → Quality, systems thinking, continuous improvement  
**Founder** → Green Digital Solutions  
**Builder** → Client platforms, internal tools, ecommerce & SaaS-style apps

## 🧠 Engineering Beyond Software

Software development isn't my only engineering background.

My professional career has included **Quality Engineering and Continuous Improvement in manufacturing**, where I've worked with production systems, process improvement, quality standards, root-cause analysis, and real-world operational problems.

That experience influences the way I write software.

**Problem**  
↓  
**Understand the system**  
↓  
**Identify the actual constraint**  
↓  
**Remove unnecessary complexity**  
↓  
**Build the solution**  
↓  
**Measure whether it worked**

It's the same mindset whether I'm troubleshooting a manufacturing process or designing an application architecture.
